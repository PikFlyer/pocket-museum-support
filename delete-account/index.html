Pocket Museum 刪除帳號

Pocket Museum（開發者 Pikflyer）的帳號可以隨時刪除。

在 App 裡刪除

  1. 打開 Pocket Museum，進入「相機櫃」。
  2. 點「我的相機」標題右邊的「…」（設定）。
  3. 點「刪除帳號」，再確認一次。iPhone 會請你用 Apple 再登入一次。

沒有 App 時

來信 pikflyer.app@gmail.com，主旨寫「Pocket Museum 刪除帳號」，並用登入時的 Apple 或 Google 電子郵件寄出。我們會在 30 天內刪除並回信通知。

會刪除的資料

  • 帳號識別碼、登入用的電子郵件與名稱。
  • 收藏紀錄：相機、盲盒、貼紙、吊飾、任務進度、拍攝紀錄與收藏憑證。

在 App 裡刪除會立即完成，不另外保留備份，刪除後無法復原。照片與影片本來就只存在你的手機，不在伺服器上；已存到系統相簿的照片請從相簿刪除。

隱私權政策

Delete your Pocket Museum account

You can delete your Pocket Museum (developer: Pikflyer) account at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Pocket Museum and go to the camera cabinet (相機櫃).
  2. Tap the “…” (Settings) next to the 我的相機 heading.
  3. Tap 刪除帳號 (Delete account) and confirm. On iPhone you will be asked to sign in with Apple once more.

Without the app

Email pikflyer.app@gmail.com with the subject "Pocket Museum account deletion", sent from the Apple or Google email you signed in with. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account identifier, sign-in email address and name.
  • Your collection records: cameras, blind boxes, stickers, charms, quest progress, capture records and item certificates.

Deleting in the app takes effect immediately; no backup is kept and it cannot be undone. Photos and videos only ever live on your phone, never on the server. Photos saved to your system library are deleted from that library.
