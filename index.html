Pocket Museum 支援

遇到問題請來信 pikflyer.app@gmail.com，附上手機型號、iOS 版本和畫面截圖。

需要帳號嗎？

不用。App 免費，沒有廣告，也沒有 App 內購買。

盲盒怎麼開？

到「盲盒倉庫」領當日的盒子，再用手指把封條橫拉撕開。

收藏不見了

收藏只存在這台手機。請先不要刪除 App；刪除 App 會一併刪除收藏與 App 內的照片。

無法拍照或存照片

到 iPhone「設定」裡的 Pocket Museum，開啟相機與照片權限。

隱私權政策

Pocket Museum Support

Email pikflyer.app@gmail.com with your phone model, iOS version and a screenshot.

Do I need an account?

No. The app is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

How do I open a blind box?

Take today's box in the blind box tab, then drag the strip across it to tear it open.

My collection is missing

Your collection exists only on this phone. Please do not delete the app: deleting it also removes your collection and the photos inside the app.

I can't take or save photos

Open Pocket Museum in the iPhone Settings app and allow Camera and Photos.
