Pocket Museum 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 9 日

資料都留在你的手機

Pocket Museum 免費版沒有帳號，也沒有自己的伺服器。收藏、照片、影片與設定都只存在這台裝置，我們看不到，也不會收集或分享。

相機與麥克風

只在你拍照或錄影時使用。照片與影片不會上傳。不允許麥克風時仍可無聲錄影。

照片圖庫

只在你選擇匯入照片，或把作品存到相簿時使用。

動作感測

用來做搖晃盲盒、反光與開箱的立體效果。資料只即時使用，不儲存也不傳送。

廣告與追蹤

沒有廣告，沒有分析工具，也不會跨 App 追蹤你。

兒童

本 App 不收集任何人的個人資料，包含兒童。

刪除資料

刪除 App 就會移除所有收藏與 App 內的照片。已存到系統相簿的照片請從相簿刪除。

變更與聯絡

之後若加入帳號或購買功能，會先更新這份說明。有任何問題，請透過 App Store 頁面的支援連結聯絡我們。

Pocket Museum Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Your data stays on your phone

The free edition of Pocket Museum has no accounts and no server of its own. Your collection, photos, videos and settings exist only on this device. We cannot see them, and we do not collect or share them.

Camera and microphone

Used only while you take a photo or record a video. Photos and videos are never uploaded. If you do not allow the microphone, you can still record without sound.

Photo library

Used only when you choose to import a photo or save your work to your library.

Motion sensors

Used for shaking blind boxes, reflections and the 3D unboxing effect. Readings are used in the moment and are neither stored nor sent.

Ads and tracking

No ads, no analytics, and no tracking across apps.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes your collection and the photos inside the app. Photos already saved to your system library are deleted from that library.

Changes and contact

If accounts or purchases are added later, this policy will be updated first. Questions: pikflyer.app@gmail.com
