Pocket Museum 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 10 日

帳號與收藏紀錄

為了讓每台相機獨一無二、不能被竄改，收藏（相機、盲盒、貼紙、吊飾與任務進度）由我們的伺服器驗證並記錄。第一次打開 App 會自動建立一個匿名帳號識別碼，不用登入就能開始玩；之後可以綁定 Apple 或 Google 帳號，換手機或重新安裝時才找得回收藏。我們保存的是帳號識別碼與收藏紀錄，不會收到你的密碼。綁定後，登入服務 Firebase 會保存 Apple 或 Google 提供的電子郵件（Apple 可以選擇隱藏），綁定 Google 另外會帶入帳號名稱，只用來管理帳號。伺服器使用 Cloudflare，身份驗證使用 Firebase。

照片與影片只存在這台裝置，不會上傳；伺服器只記錄拍攝時間與使用的相機。

1.22.0 以前的版本沒有帳號，所有資料只存在裝置上。

刪除帳號

你可以在 App 的設定裡刪除帳號，收藏紀錄會一併從伺服器刪除，無法復原。

相機與麥克風

只在你拍照或錄影時使用。照片與影片不會上傳。不允許麥克風時仍可無聲錄影。

照片圖庫

只在你選擇匯入照片，或把作品存到相簿時使用。

動作感測

用來做搖晃盲盒、反光與開箱的立體效果。資料只即時使用，不儲存也不傳送。

廣告與追蹤

沒有廣告，沒有分析工具，也不會跨 App 追蹤你。

兒童

本 App 不向兒童收集個人資料，也不是為 13 歲以下兒童設計。

刪除資料

刪除 App 會移除這台裝置上的照片。綁定過的收藏紀錄仍在帳號上；還沒綁定就刪除 App，收藏就找不回來。已存到系統相簿的照片請從相簿刪除。

變更與聯絡

之後若加入購買功能，會先更新這份說明。有任何問題，請透過 App Store 頁面的支援連結聯絡我們。

Pocket Museum Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Account and collection records

So that every camera stays one of a kind and cannot be tampered with, your collection (cameras, blind boxes, stickers, charms and quest progress) is verified and recorded by our server. The first time you open the app it creates an anonymous account identifier, so you can play without signing in. You can later link your Apple or Google account, which is what lets you recover your collection on a new phone or after reinstalling. We keep an account identifier and your collection records. We never receive your password. Once you link an account, the sign-in service, Firebase, keeps the email address Apple or Google provides (Apple lets you hide it), plus your Google account name when you link Google, used only to manage your account. The server runs on Cloudflare and sign-in uses Firebase.

Photos and videos stay on this device and are never uploaded. The server records only when a photo was taken and with which camera.

Versions before 1.22.0 have no accounts; everything stays on the device.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app settings. Your collection records are deleted from the server with it and cannot be restored.

Camera and microphone

Used only while you take a photo or record a video. Photos and videos are never uploaded. If you do not allow the microphone, you can still record without sound.

Photo library

Used only when you choose to import a photo or save your work to your library.

Motion sensors

Used for shaking blind boxes, reflections and the 3D unboxing effect. Readings are used in the moment and are neither stored nor sent.

Ads and tracking

No ads, no analytics, and no tracking across apps.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from children and is not designed for children under 13.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the photos on this device. A linked collection stays on your account; if you delete the app before linking, the collection cannot be recovered. Photos already saved to your system library are deleted from that library.
